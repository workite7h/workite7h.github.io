Welcome

Hello! I'm Workineh A. Kassie - development economist working with experience in designing and executing rigorous impact evaluation projects in Ethiopia. I have a good track record of managing complex data pipelines, coordinating research teams, and translating technical evidence into actionable policy insights for government and implementation partners. Skilled in problem-solving and cross-functional communication, I also adapt quickly to the evolving needs from industry, government and funding partners in my research endeavors. I also have a good command of statistical tools to combine administrative, satellite, and survey data for geospatial impact evaluation, and survey platforms for project operations (surveyCTO). Beyond research, I am committed to teaching, mentoring, and open science, and I enjoy building inclusive research communities that connect data, people, and real-world decision-making.
